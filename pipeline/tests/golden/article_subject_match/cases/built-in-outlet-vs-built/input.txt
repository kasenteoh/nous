Built In's weekly roundup of the largest venture deals. Northwind AI, the San Francisco research lab behind the Atlas family of language models, raised $30 billion in a round that values the company at $380 billion. The financing was led by sovereign wealth funds and existing backers. Elsewhere this week, a robotics startup raised $40 million and a cybersecurity firm closed a $25 million Series B.
